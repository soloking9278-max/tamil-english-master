Module 1 · Lesson 1 · Intermediate

Sound professional without sounding complicated.

Learn three phrases you can use at work and in an interview.

10 MIN

01 · Learn the phrases

Tamil meaning first. Then listen, pause and say the English sentence out loud.

Grammar focus: “focus on + verb-ing”“I focus on solving customer issues.” is natural. Avoid “I focus to solving customer issues.” After the preposition “on”, use the -ing form here: solving, improving, coordinating.
“on” என்பது preposition. இங்கு அதற்குப் பிறகு verb + ing: solving, improving, coordinating.

02 · Interview role-play

Listen to the question. Then answer in your own voice.

Interviewer asks
“Could you tell me about your professional experience?”
Model answer
I have extensive experience in the automotive industry. My background includes customer service, workshop coordination and inventory management. I focus on solving problems efficiently and keeping customers informed.
ஆட்டோமொபைல் துறையில் எனக்கு விரிவான அனுபவம் உள்ளது. Customer service, workshop coordination, inventory management ஆகியவற்றில் அனுபவம் உள்ளது. பிரச்சினைகளைத் திறமையாகத் தீர்த்து, வாடிக்கையாளர்களுக்குத் தொடர்ந்து தகவல் தெரிவிப்பதில் கவனம் செலுத்துகிறேன்.

Tip: speak it once while reading, once while looking away, and once from memory. This page does not record or upload your voice.
